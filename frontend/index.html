🌐 The Purpose of NEXORA V1

NEXORA is designed specifically for Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, to eliminate commuter uncertainty, prevent crowded bus stand confusion, and provide a rapid emergency safety net across the entire 35 km metropolitan radius.

  • ✓ Unified Single Platform: All 4 roles run within one responsive web application accessible anywhere worldwide.
  • ✓ Real Phone Hardware GPS: Bus drivers and passengers use genuine satellite positioning from their smartphones.
  • ✓ Zero Dummy Controls: Every button triggers an authentic action, from map pan/zoom to emergency beacon dispatch.

🗺️ Coimbatore Metropolitan Coverage

Covers all key Coimbatore zones: Gandhipuram, Peelamedu, Singanallur, Ukkadam, Ramanathapuram, Saibaba Colony, Saravanampatti, Vadavalli, Perur, Sulur, and Coimbatore International Airport.

  • ✓ Google Maps Level 21 Zoom: View individual streets, apartment blocks, and shop fronts.

🚨 What is Emergency SOS?

Emergency SOS is a high-priority distress broadcast protocol designed for commuters in danger, medical distress, or road accidents across Coimbatore.

  • 🔒 GPS Lockout Security: The SOS button is locked until genuine phone GPS coordinates are acquired. Dummy or spoofed dispatches are rejected.
  • ⏱️ 2-Step Confirmation & 3s Countdown: Clicking "ARM EMERGENCY SOS" opens a confirmation modal with a 3-second countdown to prevent accidental clicks.
  • 📡 35.0 km Whole-City Broadcast: When confirmed, coordinates are transmitted via WebSockets to all rescue units and Admin Command within the 35 km Coimbatore perimeter.

📍 Real-Time Geocoding & Life Cycle

Once triggered, the backend automatically uses OpenStreetMap Nominatim reverse-geocoding to convert raw latitude/longitude into human-readable Coimbatore street addresses (e.g. "Opposite Fun Republic Mall, Avinashi Road, Peelamedu").

  • 1 Alerted: Distress beacon active; siren sounds in responder consoles.
  • 2 Accepted: Nearest rescue unit acknowledges and claims the ticket.
  • 3 En Route: Responder initiates turn-by-turn navigation to the commuter.
  • 4 Resolved: Incident closed safely by Admin Command or responding unit.

⚡ What Does the Admin Do?

The Admin is the central city transit authority (Coimbatore City Municipal Corporation & TNSTC Traffic Operations). The Admin oversees all city operations simultaneously.

  • 🚍 Real-Time Fleet Oversight: Monitors BUS-001 speed, exact 5-decimal coordinates, and current road waypoint.
  • ⏯️ Autonomous Simulation Control: Toggles the autonomous 176-waypoint Coimbatore transit simulation (pause/resume).
  • 🚨 Emergency Incident Management: Reviews active SOS distress cards with victim details and marks them "Resolved" with 1 click.

💻 Admin Console Key Buttons

  • Pause / Resume Transit: Controls autonomous bus movement along the Gandhipuram–Airport corridor.
  • Resolve Incident: Closes an active SOS ticket and resets alert flags once rescue units assist the victim.
  • POI Filter Pills: Toggles visibility of Coimbatore hospitals, police stations, and bus stands on the master map.

🚍 What Does the Bus Driver Do?

The Driver Cockpit is designed to be mounted on the bus dashboard. It turns any driver's smartphone into a real-time satellite transit beacon.

  • 📡 Real Satellite Hardware GPS: Reads native phone geolocation and streams vehicle latitude, longitude, and speed every 2 seconds.
  • ▶ Trip Controls: Tapping "START TRIP" locks onto BUS-001 and begins live route broadcasting. Tapping "COMPLETE TRIP" ends the route safely.
  • 🛣️ Corridor Headway: Alerts driver to distance traveled and stops along the Coimbatore transit corridor.

📱 Driver Console Key Buttons

  • ▶ START TRIP / ⏹ COMPLETE TRIP: Starts or ends active bus telemetry broadcasting.
  • POI Highlights (Hospitals, Police, Bus Stands): Toggles emergency landmark overlays on driver navigation.

🧭 What Does the Passenger Do?

The Passenger Web App is used by daily Coimbatore commuters waiting at bus stops or traveling on buses. It works directly in mobile browsers without downloading any app.

  • 🗺️ Live Bus Proximity: Displays moving BUS-001 in real time along Avinashi Road and Trichy Road.
  • ⏱️ Haversine Arrival Countdown: Computes walking distance (km) and estimated arrival time (minutes) from commuter's exact phone GPS position.
  • 🚨 2-Step Emergency SOS: Allows commuter to transmit high-priority distress alerts with exact GPS coordinates in danger.

📱 Passenger App Key Buttons

  • ⚠️ GPS REQUIRED / 🚨 ARM EMERGENCY SOS: Unlocks once GPS is acquired; opens confirmation modal.
  • Confirm Emergency Dispatch (3s): Finalizes distress transmission after 3-second countdown.
  • Cancel Emergency (False Alarm): Cancels ticket if triggered by accident.

🚒 What Does the Rescue Responder Do?

Responders (police patrol cars, 108 ambulances, and fire rescue units) monitor the 35.0 km Coimbatore emergency radar perimeter.

  • 🚨 Emergency Siren & Alert Feed: Plays audible synthesized siren immediately when any commuter activates SOS in Coimbatore.
  • 📍 Victim Intelligence: Displays commuter name, phone number, exact GPS coordinates, and reverse-geocoded street address.
  • 🗺️ 1-Tap Google Navigation: Clicks button to open turn-by-turn driving directions to the victim's location.

🚒 Responder Key Buttons

  • Navigate with Google Maps: Launches external Google Maps driving directions directly to victim's coordinates.
  • Acknowledge SOS: Advances ticket state from "Alerted" to "Accepted".
  • Mark En Route: Signals to victim and Admin that rescue vehicle is actively traveling.
