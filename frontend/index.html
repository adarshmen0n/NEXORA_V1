🚍 PART 1: DRIVER COCKPIT
🚍 Vehicle Controller & Beacon

Real GNSS Hardware Satellite Telemetry

The driver cockpit communicates directly with the smartphone's internal GNSS sensor via high-accuracy HTML5 geolocation. Telemetry is streamed securely via WebSockets to our central FastAPI server every 2 seconds.

  • 1 Trip Lifecycle Management: Driver taps "Start Trip" to lock onto Bus BUS-001 and initialize telemetry recording.
  • 2 Continuous Satellite Ingest: Acquires latitude, longitude, transit speed, and heading with ±5m precision.
  • 3 Autonomous Fallback Engine: In demo mode, our 176-waypoint Coimbatore corridor simulation keeps the bus active 24/7.
📱 PART 2: COMMUTER RADAR
📱 Commuter Mobility & SOS

Predictive Arrival ETA & Strict 2-Step SOS

Commuters monitor the approaching bus on a Google Roadmap vector view with dynamic ETA calculations based on actual road distance and real vehicle velocity.

  • 1 Live Haversine Radar: Recalculates distance and arrival minutes every 2 seconds as the bus progresses.
  • 2 Zero-Tolerance Safety Lockout: Emergency SOS button remains disarmed until a genuine GPS satellite fix is locked.
  • 3 3-Second Confirmation Barrier: Prevents accidental triggers; displays exact victim coordinates before broadcast.
🚨 PART 3: RESCUE DISPATCH
🚨 Tactical Rescue Operations

Whole-City 35.0 km Emergency Radar

Emergency rescue units (police, ambulance, fire) receive instant distress dispatches across the entire Coimbatore metropolitan area (35 km radius) with audible alerts and automated street address geocoding.

  • 1 Instant Audio Alarm: Synthesized dual-tone siren alerts responders immediately upon ticket generation.
  • 2 Nominatim Street Geocoding: Resolves raw coordinates into human-readable Coimbatore street addresses in real time.
  • 3 4-Stage Ticket Lifecycle: Track incidents through Alerted ➔ Acknowledged ➔ En Route ➔ Safe.
🖥️ PART 4: INCIDENT COMMAND
🖥️ Central Operations Command

Fleet Telemetry & Metropolitan Incident Desk

City transit authorities oversee all active buses, commuter densities, and emergency incidents simultaneously on a unified operations dashboard with real-time incident resolution controls.

  • 1 Real-Time Coordinates Ticker: Displays 5-decimal-place GPS telemetry, speed, and active transit sector.
  • 2 City Incident Desk: Review open emergency tickets, victim phone numbers, and dispatch units with 1 click.
  • 3 Autonomous Simulation Control: Pause or resume citywide bus movement simulation on the fly.
